Privacy policy

Mælaborð · last updated 30 September 2026

Who this applies to

Mælaborð is a personal dashboard used by a single person, its owner. It runs locally on the owner's own computer and cannot be reached from the internet. No one else can sign in to it.

What Google data it accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, Mælaborð asks for read-only access to:

  • Sleep: sleep sessions and sleep stages (googlehealth.sleep.readonly)
  • Health metrics: resting heart rate, heart rate variability and weight (googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly)
  • Activity: steps, calories, active zone minutes and exercise sessions (googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly)
  • Calendar: calendar events (calendar.readonly)

Mælaborð never creates, changes or deletes anything in the Google account.

Bank account data

The owner can also connect their own bank account (Landsbankinn) through Enable Banking, a licensed account information service provider under the EU/EEA Payment Services Directive (PSD2). Access is read-only: account names, balances and transactions. It cannot make payments. Consent is given with the owner's electronic ID at the bank and expires automatically (at most 180 days); it can be withdrawn at any time in Mælaborð or at the bank. Transactions are stored only in the local database on the owner's computer and are used only to show the owner their own spending, budget and savings rate.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner charts and summaries of their own sleep, health, activity, schedule and spending. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

How the data is stored

Data from Google and the bank, and the access tokens used to fetch it, are stored in a local database file on the owner's computer. Mælaborð has no server of its own: the only network requests it makes for this data go directly to Google's APIs and to Enable Banking's API. The page banki.html on this site only forwards the owner's browser back to their own computer after signing in at the bank; it stores nothing.

Sharing

Google user data and bank data are not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party.

Limited Use

Mælaborð's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data is kept on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it.
  • Choosing “Aftengja” (disconnect) in Mælaborð revokes its access to the Google account and deletes the stored tokens.
  • Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting Mælaborð's local database removes all stored data.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on Google's sign-in screen for Mælaborð.
