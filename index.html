Mælaborð

A private, personal dashboard. Persónulegt mælaborð.

Mælaborð is a personal dashboard that runs only on its owner's own computer. It brings study, training, nutrition, personal finances, calendar and health data together in one place, so the owner can follow their own goals.

How it uses Google data

With the owner's permission, Mælaborð reads (read-only):

  • sleep, activity and health measurements from the Google Health API (Fitbit), and
  • events from Google Calendar,

and shows them as charts and summaries on the owner's computer. The data is stored only on that computer. It is never shared, sold or sent anywhere else.

Not a public service

Mælaborð has one user, its owner. It has no sign-up and cannot be reached from the internet; it runs at localhost on the owner's own machine.
